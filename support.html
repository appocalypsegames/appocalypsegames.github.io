How do I report a problem?

Email us with these details so we can take a closer look:

  • The game name and version.
  • Your device and operating system.
  • What happened and the steps to reproduce it.
  • A screenshot, if you have one.
Where can I find your games?

Visit our games, choose a title, and follow the links to its official stores. Each game page lists its platforms.

Can I share feedback or an idea?

Absolutely. Send your thoughts to appocalypse333@gmail.com. We love hearing about your experience with our games.
