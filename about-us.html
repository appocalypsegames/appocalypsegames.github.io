Hello. We’re Appocalypse.

Small by choice.
Curious by nature.

We’re an independent game studio from Salamanca, Spain. Since 2016, we’ve been making games that follow our curiosity. Wherever it takes us.

Our way of making games

Good games start
with a “what if?”.

For us, making games is about exploring an idea and seeing how far it can go. Sometimes that means swinging a bowling ball through space. Sometimes it means finding out what’s hiding below a football pitch.

Our games take different shapes, but they share the same spirit: a love of play, a taste for the unexpected, and the freedom to try something different.
